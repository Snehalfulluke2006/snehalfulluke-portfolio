## Visual Storytelling Redefined

Neon City is a technical study in blending real-world elements with computer-generated environments. By using Unreal Engine's real-time rendering, I was able to iterate on complex lighting setups in seconds rather than hours.

### Technical Breakdown

The project involved complex camera tracking and rotoscoping to seamlessly integrate live-action actors into a futuristic cityscape. The final color grade was crafted to evoke a 'cinematic dark' aesthetic.
