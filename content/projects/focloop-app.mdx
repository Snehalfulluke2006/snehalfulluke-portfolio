## Deep Work Unleashed

Focloop is designed for high-performance individuals who need to protect their attention. The app doesn't just block notifications; it analyzes your productivity patterns using cognitive heatmaps.

### Engineering Challenges

Implementing real-time biometric synchronization required low-latency data streams between the mobile device and the Supabase backend. I utilized Riverpod for predictable state management across the entire application.
